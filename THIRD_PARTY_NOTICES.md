# TurboFace third-party notices

TurboFace's public source repository has client-specific notice files because
the Classic Era and WoW Forever packages contain slightly different adapted
material:

- [Classic Era notices](src/classic/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)
- [WoW Forever notices](src/forever/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)

The corresponding license and attribution texts are stored in
[`src/common/Licenses/`](src/common/Licenses/) and are included under
`TurboFace/Licenses/` in every generated release package.

These third-party materials are governed by their respective licenses and are
not covered by the All Rights Reserved restrictions that apply to TurboFace's
original material.
