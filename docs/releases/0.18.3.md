# TurboFace 0.18.3

TurboFace 0.18.3 focuses on Forever aura reliability, restored target combat
features, trainer-data correctness, and safer quest automation. Classic Era is
rebuilt from the same verified unified source and retains its client-specific
behavior.

## Highlights

- Restored Target Buff, Target Debuff, and Target-of-Target Debuff mover content
  on Forever through Blizzard's supported secret-aware AuraContainer API.
- Unified countdown, stack-count, font, and cooldown-swipe presentation across
  player, target, party, pet, and nameplate aura surfaces.
- Fixed Player buff/debuff styling and cooldown swipes disappearing during
  combat or transient secret-value transitions.
- Corrected Player aura layering so cooldown swipes darken the icon without
  darkening duration or stack text.
- Centered detached Forever nameplate aura rows against the complete nameplate
  rather than the narrower health bar.
- Restored the Forever Target Swing Timer with a client-owned combat cadence
  estimator while keeping the incomplete Forever cast-bar path disabled.
- Fixed profession recipes such as Barbecued Buzzard Wing leaking into class
  training and learned ranked abilities such as Rend remaining in the queue.
- Serialized quest and gossip automation behind later frames and success events
  to prevent intermittent stuck NPC interactions.
- Expanded regression and architecture verification for the new provider and
  client-policy contracts.

Portable SavedVariables remain at schema version 79.
